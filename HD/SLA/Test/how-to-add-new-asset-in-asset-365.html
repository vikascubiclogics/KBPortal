How to Add a New Asset in Asset 365

Asset 365 allows administrators and authorized users to maintain a centralized inventory of organizational assets such as laptops, desktops, monitors, mobile devices, accessories, and other equipment.


This article explains how to add a new asset in Asset 365.

Before you begin

• Asset 365 is installed and configured on your SharePoint site.
• You have permission to create or manage assets.
• The required asset categories and asset types are already configured.
• Employee information is available if the asset needs to be assigned immediately.
• You have the required asset information, such as:

Steps

1
Open Asset 365

Navigate to the SharePoint site where Asset 365 is installed.

Open Asset 365 from the application page or navigation menu.

After saving the asset:

  1. Open the Assets page.
  2. Search for the newly created asset using its Asset Name, Asset ID, or Serial Number.
  3. Open the asset record.
  4. Confirm that the asset details are correct.
  5. If the asset was assigned to an employee, verify that the employee information is displayed correctly.
  6. Confirm that the asset status reflects the expected state, such as Available, Assigned, or another configured status.

Troubleshooting

The Add Asset button is not visible

Check that:

  • All mandatory fields are completed.
  • The Asset ID or Asset Tag is unique.
  • The entered values are in the required format.
  • Your SharePoint session is active.

Refresh the page and try again if necessary.

Employee is not available in the Assigned To field

Verify that:

  • The employee exists in the configured employee directory.
  • The employee account is active.
  • Asset 365 has access to retrieve the employee information.

Contact your administrator if the employee is still unavailable.
